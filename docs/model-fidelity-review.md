# Hollow Saint visual refinement

The user rejected blockout 01 and requested continued modeling/review loops.
Visual fidelity to selected LEFT A is the goal. Counts and static validity
alone cannot prove completion. No change to the confirmed gameplay scope.

## V1 evidence and correction priorities

Independent silhouette review refined the diagnosis: selected A hips are near
60% of body height and knees near 38%, versus roughly 53% and 29% in v1.
Raise the hips/knees and widen the stance before evaluating surface detail.
At a 2 m body scale, target hips near 1.20 m, knees near 0.75 m and ankles
near x = +/-0.38 m. These are reference-derived construction targets.
Halo diameter is already approximately 44% of body height in both references
and v1; the lower-ring placement, rather than diameter alone, is the mismatch.

1. Neck reads as a straight exposed post and head as a faceted diamond. Replace
   with a short layered neck and continuous elongated porcelain mask surface.
2. Chest consists of disconnected polygon wedges around an oversized flat
   emitter. Shape enclosing chest shells around a small recessed circular core;
   add dark rib/abdomen anatomy so the waist belongs to the same body.
3. Halo reads as a broad target behind the whole torso. Match concept framing
   around head/shoulders and use narrow bevels and four distinct copper sections.
4. Forearms, thighs and shins read as tubes with flat cutoffs. Build curved,
   tapered shells with diagonal edges and dark joint exposure like the concepts.
5. Feet are rectangular stubs, hands thin parallel rods. Model articulated
   dark hands with tapered curled digits and ceramic split-toe foot forms.
6. Tabard became a short plain rectangle. Restore the long narrow layered strip
   seen in selected A, with light drape and simple copper ring/line motif.
7. Material transitions need ivory ceramic, dark mechanical joints and aged
   copper; soften bevel highlights while preserving broad readable facets.

## Render/review loop

- Preserve each saved pass; use original image as reference, never only the
  previous mesh or a newly generated image that may change the design.
- Inspect front, side, rear and three-quarter views with neutral studio light.
- Compare relative head/shoulder/waist/hip proportions and limb silhouettes;
  distinguish pose/lighting effects from actual geometric corrections.
- Check details up close, then player-distance rear readability.
- Record concrete remaining mismatches and implement another targeted pass.
- Keep the editable .blend and build sources together. No generated mockup may
  substitute for a render of the actual delivered mesh.

## Completion evidence

The actual saved model must preserve the selected slender humanoid, blank
ivory mask, shaped ceramic armor, dark articulated joints/hands, cyan core,
copper halo and long dark tabard. It must visibly improve the deficiencies
above and withstand comparison from multiple angles. Editable geometry must
reopen successfully. No rig, animation, export or game-readiness claim follows
from this visual milestone. User acceptance remains unproven until provided.

## Pass v2: observed results

Actual front, hero and side Blender renders inspected. The longer legs, wider
stance, compressed articulated abdomen, smaller core, curved blank mask and
long tabard improve resemblance substantially over v1. Completion is unproven:

- Dark foot base protrudes through the ivory instep as a conspicuous band.
- Shoulder joints break through the ceramic caps in front/hero views.
- Halo was moved too far upward, leaving excessive space above the head.
- Limb armor still has straight horizontal tube-like cutoffs at some joints.
- Tabard is long but too planar/regular; fingers need a more natural gesture.
- Side view exposes several accents that appear detached from the body.

Pass v3 targets these issues. Original LEFT A depicts a bottom-open halo while
the detail study shows a complete four-quarter ring. The complete ring was an
unapproved blockout inference. Shortening the lower two arcs while keeping four
segments is now being evaluated to match the selected full-body silhouette.
This is a visual construction revision, not a new character selection.

## Passes v3 and v4: observed results

V3 replaced the concave chest caps with curved quad ribbons, concealed shoulder
joint penetrations, split the ceramic toes, curled/fanned the digits and opened
the bottom of the four-segment halo. V4 removed the stepped dark thigh-top
intersection and made the mask seam visibly continuous. Actual front, hero and
side renders inspected; all five cameras rendered for each saved version.

Remaining visual problems identified before the next pass:

- Side view shows chest-shell gaps and cyan throat/back accents offset from
  the underlying surfaces. Attach accents after final proportion changes.
- Paired rounded toe lobes resemble slippers; the concept has a stronger
  angular instep, articulated dark ankle and smaller ceramic toe tips.
- The upper shin transitions through a straight horizontal ledge; shape a
  flowing knee cutout and longitudinal ceramic ridge.
- Dark upper arms and abdomen need tapered tendons and nested V forms,
  rather than straight cylinders and horizontal stacks.
- Finger knuckles/proximal segments need more volume relative to the tips.

These are geometry and material corrections. Improved lighting or generated
image retouching would not establish that the actual model has improved.

## Passes v5/v6 and supplied Higgsfield model

V5 fixed side attachment gaps, seated conductors and improved knee transitions.
V6 adds rear hamstring/calf forms, copper variation, a smaller dark core border,
and restores the front tabard motif lost during v5 surface seating. Independent
review finds the local study now clearly resembles LEFT A enough for review.
V6 saved-scene integrity passed; no production or game readiness established.

The user then supplied a Higgsfield SAM3D GLB from another agent. Same-studio
renders and clay inspection show it is substantially closer in body contours,
mask/neck, armor openings and feet. Recommendation is a pivot to its body as
the visual base, adapting our editable halo/hands and correcting the generated
back. [Full comparison and concrete next pass](../art/comparison/comparison.md).
The recommendation is now implemented in [hybrid v7](../art/hybrid/README.md).
Both sources are preserved. See [hybrid review evidence](hybrid-model-review.md)
for the actual five-view visual audit and saved-file integrity results.
