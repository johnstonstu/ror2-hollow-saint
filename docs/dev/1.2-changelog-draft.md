# 1.2.0 changelog draft (player-facing)

Draft for Stu. Assumes hold/release Gaze wins the A/B. Replaces the private-candidate notes at the top of `HollowSaintMod/Package/CHANGELOG.md` when 1.2 ships. Numbers are defaults.

## 1.2.0
- **Gaze of the Hollow reworked.** Hover and channel the beam for 7 seconds. Hold Primary to load up to 3 stored Static Charges (one every 0.28 s), then release to fire them down the beam as one surge for 400% damage per charge. Release early to spend fewer. Surges land almost instantly, proc items, knock enemies back and heal 1% of max health per charge. Unspent charges return when the beam ends.
- **Gaze charge-up.** Hold Special before the beam to pull your stored Static Charges into the crown (each one spirals in with its own rising power-up sound), aiming as you gather. When the beam opens it fires them all at once: the crown flares wide and kicks back, a wave rides down the beam, and it lands with a blast for 600% damage per charge that grows wider with every charge. Tap Special to skip the charge-up. Gaze armor now also covers the charge-up.
- Minor Gaze surges also lock a lightning bolt onto the closest enemy within 14 m (200% damage per charge, small splash), so anything closing in under the hover gets hit while you aim elsewhere. Skipped when the surge is already landing on it.
- Gaze feedback: the beam thickens and flashes with each loaded charge and flares on release; each charge has its own rising chime; the surge's impact has its own sound, camera kick and lightning, bigger per charge.
- Gaze controls: Special or UI Cancel ends the channel; Utility exits and activates if ready.
- Gaze balance: beam proc coefficient 0.5 to 0.3, fork damage 100% to 70%, splash proc 0.3 to 0.2 (the beam lasts longer than before). Saved default values migrate; custom values are kept.
- **Answered Prayer** stores Static Charges instead of discharging automatically. A full bank turns your next successful Stormspear throw into a Thunderbolt on impact.
- **Open Circuit**: the crown opens to the edge of its damage area. Its lightning now crawls across the ground almost to that edge, climbing rocks and stopping at walls and drops. Enemies that stay inside for 3 seconds take one 270% zap (now with its own sound).
- Damage: non-Gaze skill damage reduced 10%. Stormspear recharge 5 to 6 seconds.
- **Crimson Vow**, a new mastery skin (win on Monsoon).
- **Stormspear impact** now bursts in 3D: a dome of lightning, a lance of light out of the impact, a shockwave ring at the real damage radius and a spark fountain, all scaling with charge (a tap still splashes).
- Visual cleanup: smaller chain-lightning hit flashes that no longer hide enemies, ground rings that follow slopes, tinted sparks, a softer Arc Step afterimage.
- Audio mix pass: Arc Bolt, spear throw, Arc Step and the Thunderbolt-ready cue are easier to hear; the spear charge loop is quieter.
- New Mod Options for Gaze: charge speed, surge damage and surge proc.
- Gaze text updated in all four languages.
