# Hollow Saint 1.2 Gaze and kit refinement

Gaze's stored charges currently buy sustained beam power much more than immediate impact. The strongest next experiment is to move some of that power into deliberate aimed surges, while retaining the hover, electrical beam identity and familiar trigger action. This is a prototype proposal, not accepted release tuning.

## Direction from Stuart

- Preserve movement overall; consider only a small increase during Gaze.
- Charging is acceptable, but spending charges should feel intuitive and powerful.
- Avoid an opening sequence of repeated trigger presses followed by a long passive sweep.
- Blend the original Gaze VFX and SFX with the newer pulse presentation.
- Evaluate random item builds as well as controlled loadouts before settling damage.
- Preserve the improving Stormspear and Arc Bolt. Polish Circuit audio, terrain contact and white crown artifacts.

## Why immediate charge spending is attractive

At the default capacity of five, one launched charge gives:

| Effect | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Direct pulse strike | 50% body damage per eligible enemy; at most eight distinct victims |
| Ordinary core tick | 100% body damage before launch ramp |
| Pulse item proc | Zero; the pulse calls TakeDamage under the Storm guard without ReportHit |
| Duration | Adds two seconds, up to fourteen seconds total |
| Lasting damage ramp | Adds 5% ordinary beam, splash and fork/chain damage, up to 25% |
| Recovery | Claims 1% of snapshotted maximum health per charge at default capacity, up to 5%; native healing items can modify it |
| Input spacing | Fresh presses at least 0.25 seconds apart |
| Press to direct impact | 0.32 seconds intake plus 0.35–0.55 seconds travel, before network delay |

Five accepted presses can therefore occur in about one second; the fifth launch follows at about 1.32 seconds after the first press. Spending earlier earns the lasting beam ramp earlier and opens reserve capacity earlier. There are situational reasons to wait, including future targets, wider later ground reach, healing and retaining unused fuel, but those are much less explicit than the guaranteed beam benefits. Calling this a charging meter also obscures that each press already spends ammunition.

Attack speed raises ordinary beam tick frequency without raising pulse damage or shortening the manual input interval. The pulse therefore loses relative damage importance in fast attack-speed builds. Increasing pulse loudness alone would leave that underlying mismatch.

## Analytical damage comparison

Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/analysis/Measure-Gaze120.ps1`. It reads the pinned candidate's source and produces JSON. These are continuous-time estimates, not measured game DPS: level one, perfect continuous aim at one enemy, five entry charges, all direct pulses landing, no crits, forks, splash, passive damage or item effects. Fixed-step timing can change actual tick totals.

| Attack speed | Pulse schedule | Core damage | Direct pulse damage | Pulse share of these two sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1x | Five presses 0.25 s apart | 8647.5% | 250% | 2.81% |
| 1x | Five presses 2 s apart | 8210% | 250% | 2.96% |
| 2x | Five presses 0.25 s apart | 17295% | 250% | 1.42% |
| 4x | Five presses 0.25 s apart | 34590% | 250% | 0.72% |

The core cadence floor is 0.05 seconds: this model stops gaining ticks above 4x attack speed. With no charges, the level-one four-second core contributes approximately 2000% at 1x. All five direct pulse hits together contribute only 250%; most of the full-bank advantage comes from longer beam exposure and the ramp. This comparison explains incentives, not encounter balance. Multi-target coverage and item damage require live measurement.

## Prototype comparison

| Prototype | Change | What it can answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Presentation control | Keep current mechanics; restore some original beam presence; separate intake, launch and hit cues | How much of the dissatisfaction is presentation alone? It deliberately leaves the early-spend incentive intact. |
| B Deliberate surges | Trial a fixed 6–8 second channel, independent of entry fuel; remove the permanent per-spend damage ramp; place more of the damage budget in discrete aimed surges with approximately 0.8–1.0 seconds recovery | Can target choice and timing make the trigger feel like an attack throughout the cast? Recommended first mechanical experiment. |
| C Hold and release | Hold Primary to prepare one larger discharge, release to fire; retain the continuous beam underneath | Could one deliberate release feel better? It adds another charging interaction and risks slowing aim, so test only if B remains unsatisfying. |

These durations and recovery times are trial ranges. Do not simply add stronger pulses to the fourteen-second ramping version. Calibrate per-cast damage, full cast-plus-cooldown throughput and peak damage separately. A shorter cast can reduce total damage while increasing practical safety and availability.

For B, preserve ordinary zero-fuel casting, entry/reserve separation, unspent-fuel return, server authority, mapped cancellation and native utility exit. Keep the existing healing budget during the first comparison so healing does not become another changed variable. A missed shot should visibly miss; do not add hidden target attraction or damaging ground effects that ignore geometry. Keep attack-speed scaling as a separate experiment rather than removing item synergy without measurement.

Try drift 0.30 versus 0.33 separately from the surge change. Test beam steering with normal controller input on close targets before changing the hover height. Scripted aim reaches steeply downward; this does not establish that the camera and right stick make it comfortable.

## Gaze presentation and HUD

The current beam deliberately dims its sheath, core, helices and tether during charge presentation. Restore enough of the earlier continuous beam to preserve its identity, then give each surge a brief intake, sharp launch and distinct confirmed impact. Match the strongest impact cue to the damage arrival, rather than presenting the launch sound as proof of a hit. Keep startup, continuous hum and release identity recognizable.

The current timer bar resets its full scale on each extension, and its five pips count launches already spent. For B, use one stable duration bar and remaining-charge indicators that count down. Keep reserve gains visually subordinate and distinct from ammunition available now. Confirm readability in the native gameplay HUD at 1080p and 1440p with controller glyphs; staged render-camera screenshots omit the HUD.

Riot's first-party [Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) describes aligning visual/audio emphasis with gameplay importance, preserving a clear hierarchy and limiting accumulated noise. Applied here, that supports making a surge a distinct meaningful event instead of increasing brightness across every layer. It supplies design guidance, not RoR2 damage targets.

## Controlled and random item tests

The existing `DevAutopilot.Items.cs` combines primary, spear, utility and special into each tally. Its Gaze sequence holds Primary rather than repeatedly requesting the new pulses. Entry fuel is not standardized; packs can be killed before the special begins. It is useful for integration smoke checks, but those aggregate totals cannot isolate the current Gaze's balance.

Before comparing builds, add a dev-only measurement segment with explicit success criteria:

1. Each trial records candidate hash, game build, active mod list, config, level, actual inventory, attack speed, target layout, entry/reserve fuel and input schedule. Same recorded conditions can be replayed against both candidates.
2. Separate ordinary core, splash, forks, fueled pulse, Electrocute, spear/conductor and item damage. Do not infer source from the broad Special damage tag alone. Record accepted presses, actual launches, confirmed hits and returns.
3. Reset bodies, inventory, enemies, charges, temporary buffs and outstanding projectiles between trials. Verify the actual inventory after void conversions. Wait for the channel to finish before removing a loadout.
4. Run no-item, attack-speed, proc-chain, burst/band, crit/healing, cooldown/stock, and on-kill groups first. Include both single targets and packs; on-kill output needs fresh killable enemies.
5. Then generate and save seeded mixed inventories at early, mid and late item budgets. Weight them plausibly by tier and stack distribution, keep lunar/void-heavy stress builds separate, and replay identical manifests against both versions. Initially use ten manifests per budget and three repeats for a screening pass; widen sampling when differences approach run-to-run variance.
6. Report time to kill, direct versus item damage share, damage per cast and per complete cooldown cycle, fuel generation/spending, discarded inputs, nearby-target tracking and frame-time percentiles. Random trials are coverage, not proof of universal balance.

The first mechanical prototype succeeds only if individual pulse hits are recognizable in clutter, accurate aiming has a visible payoff, and immediate five-charge dumping is no longer required to reach the advertised state. It must remain useful with zero fuel and preserve existing cleanup/stock semantics. Reject a timing design that turns controller input into mandatory rhythmic tapping or makes common item builds feel ineffective.

Item-specific rules must be checked against the installed game. Official [December 9 2025 patch notes](https://support.2k.com/hc/en-us/articles/47210814399123-Risk-of-Rain-2-Patch-Notes-December-9-2025) include proc-related exceptions such as Hiker's Boots losing proc-coefficient dependence and Voidsent Flame self-proccing on full-health targets. A universal proc multiplier is therefore not a sufficient item model. The old project proc audit also predates this candidate and must not be treated as its current specification.

## Circuit and the rest of the kit

Keep Circuit's damage behavior fixed while diagnosing its visuals. Current ground branches use eight samples with 0.35-metre steps, beginning about 0.25 metres from the player: roughly 2.7 metres of path, much shorter than the eight-metre damage radius. Extending their terrain sampling and checking the damage-sphere intersection could make the crown feel connected to more surrounding ground. Preserve clipped valid prefixes and bounded world queries rather than drawing across walls or voids.

White spinning artifacts require distinguishing metal material highlights, lightning cores, bloom and mesh overlap. Compare bright/dark terrain and affected skins, then isolate layers in a private diagnostic build. Do not declare the cause from a still image. Give Circuit its own restrained continuous bed and distinguish ordinary pulses from its stronger dwell zap. Gaze pulse SFX changes do not by themselves constitute a Circuit sound pass.

Recheck Stormspear tap/full hits, burst, conductor and funded/unfunded crown strikes separately. Preserve the recent hit/flight improvements. Review Arc Bolt with high attack speed for impact clutter and off-hand interaction; do not stack another global damage reduction on top of its existing runtime factor.
