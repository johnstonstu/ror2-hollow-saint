# 1.3.0 detailed dev changelog (pre-release passes, superseded values included)

## 1.3.0
- **Playtest rework (Oct 7 night):** Hollowed Orb hits harder (297% per hit, 3 hits free; up to 657% and 8 hits), latches onto a lone enemy to zap its remaining hits, then bursts; the player bounce is gone. Thundercloud is a free-to-cast lingering storm (3 s free, +1 s per charge) striking everything beneath it every 0.75 s for 81-162%, Shocking and priming. Closed Circuit leftovers burst as a 12 m crown nova (150% per charge) instead of sky strikes. Gaze opening blast 600% to 400% per charge; lock-on bolt 200% to 100% per charge within 8 m. Storm income log is always printed.
- **Storm loop:** every skill that spends charges now primes what it hits (Gaze opening blasts, surges and lock-on strikes, and the full-bank Thunderbolt's splash join Hollowed Orb and Thundercloud). Finishers tip primed enemies into Electrocutes. See the new loop diagram and build-outs in the README.
- **Closed Circuit:** charges fed into Open Circuit return one per Electrocute within 12 m. Any still in the crown when it closes fall as 270% lightning strikes on enemies within 30 m, primed ones first, with a closing ground telegraph; with nothing in range they return to the bank. Empowered pulses now build Static on primed enemies.
- **Stormspear is the Secondary finisher:** its hits build 2x Static on primed enemies, and the full-bank Thunderbolt now only spends the bank on a fully charged throw, so quick throws never empty it. A fully charged spear with a full bank flashes and chimes to show it will spend.
- **Hollowed Orb is the Secondary primer:** a free tap primes everything it bounces through.
- **Income guard:** at most 2 Static Charges bank per second (bursts of two still land at once); Electrocutes past it still stun, Shock and arc. New log line `HOLLOW_SAINT_STORM_INCOME` reports charges per minute, primes, refunds, closing strikes and wasted charges.
- All new values are in Mod Options: Closed Circuit, refund reach, closing strike damage/range/priming, empowered pulses finishing primed, Gaze priming, Thunderbolt splash priming, charge income per second, spear primed multiplier and full-charge Thunderbolt.
- **Storm flow:** Hollowed Orb and Thundercloud hits now prime Static on their targets (Orb 35% on a fresh hit, less on revisits; cloud 35%), capped at 95% so they never Electrocute by themselves. Finish primed enemies with Arc Bolt, Stormspear or Open Circuit to bank charges back. Thundercloud strikes also leave victims Shocked. All three values are configurable.
- Charge gathering for Hollowed Orb, Open Circuit and Thundercloud now ticks every 0.25 seconds (was 0.30). Orb's free short-cast window is unchanged at 0.5 seconds.
- Thundercloud sits higher and flatter so it frames the fight instead of covering the upper view, and each strike briefly lights the cloud from inside.
- Rewrote Hollowed Orb, Thundercloud, Open Circuit, passive and survivor descriptions in all four languages: shorter, with the priming loop explained. Orb shows the Static keyword; Thundercloud shows Shocked and Static.
- Thundercloud now rolls across targets over roughly 4–6 seconds with four branching visual return strokes per victim and rounder cloud billows. Damage still occurs once per enemy; its cooldown starts after the longer sequence.
- Hollowed Orb bounce reach scales from 18 m free/one charge to 27 m at three and 36 m at five. Added a configurable per-charge reach increment; hit budgets, damage and harmless player relay remain unchanged.
- Early-game Arc Bolt damage increases from 108% to 144% effective damage at the same cadence, chain reach and proc coefficients. Exact old-default migration 15 preserves custom damage settings.
- Hollowed Orb can bounce harmlessly through its nearby owner when one or two reachable enemies remain. Fresh enemies take priority; player contacts cause no damage, procs, Static or extra enemy hits. The original finite budget and diminishing repeat damage remain.
- Updated 1.3 player documentation, Static exceptions and legacy Gaze/ordinary Thunderbolt configuration descriptions.
- Hollowed Orb now crackles around its spherical surface while gathering and bouncing, with short trailing bolts in flight. Impacts add radial lightning, small sparks, expanding shock rings and throttled electrical audio layers in each skin's colors.
- First-playtest refinement: Hollowed Orb now works without Static (157.5% damage, two hits). Short casts preserve the bank; holds past 0.5 seconds gather charges for the existing empowered tiers. Improved reticle-first aim assistance, quicker release, substantial base charging ball and bright body/arm energy feeds.
- Open Circuit now gathers at least one stored charge before opening. Extra charges increase area pulse density and crown lightning, up to twice the baseline frequency at five; extra pulses do not accelerate direct Static generation. Its overhead Orb cast leaves Primary attacks available.
- **Thundercloud**, a third Special selection: hold to gather stored Static Charges into the crown, then release a broad aimed storm. The crown rises into a cloud, strikes each eligible enemy once in a rolling sequence, and fades. Its radius grows from 16 to 30 m and damage from 270% to 720% with one to five charges.
- **Hollowed Orb**, an alternate Secondary: gather charges between both hands and throw a large electrical ball. One to five charges increase its diameter from 0.6 to 1 m, first-hit damage from 225% to 495%, and budget from 3 to 7 total hits. It prioritizes fresh enemies, then bounces between remaining targets with diminishing damage and reduced item procs.
- Thundercloud requires at least one stored charge; Hollowed Orb can use charges for extra power. Release early to keep unused charges; Utility cancels gathering into Arc Step. Direct hits from these spenders do not refill their own charge bank.
- During **Open Circuit**, Hollowed Orb gathers and launches above the head like the crown spear. World clearance prevents the cast point moving through a ceiling.
- New skill icons, skin-colored effects, rising charge sounds, mapped skill controls, configurable balance and descriptions in all four languages.
- Stormspear remains the default Secondary. Gaze and Open Circuit remain the first two Special selections. Stormspear and Gaze balance is preserved; Open Circuit adds charge-powered pulse density.
- Physical controller acceptance and a real multiplayer playtest remain unverified.
