# Hollow Saint 1.2 balance review (read-only audit)

Date: 2026-10-05. Scope: worktree `claude-120` (Gaze hold/release trial on by default). Nothing was run in game. All "code" numbers were read from source; all "vanilla" numbers are recollection and are tagged (V). Where I am unsure of a vanilla value it is marked `?`.

Conventions: coefficients are x body damage (1.00 = 100%). "Effective" = after `KitDamagePolicy.NonGazeMultiplier = 0.9` (applied once to Arc Bolt, Stormspear direct, Open Circuit pulse/dwell, Thunderbolt; NOT to Gaze, Electrocute pop, chain/splash fractions, Shocked, item procs). DPS figures are %/s of base damage at 1x attack speed, single target, perfect uptime unless stated.

Hollow Saint is a Commando-class body with +15 armor, +10% speed and a free air jump. Slightly sturdier than Commando/Artificer/Huntress, not an outlier.

## 2. Skill-by-skill (code values, 1x attack speed)

### Primary: Arc Bolt (`KitTuning`, `ArcBoltState`, `ArcBoltChain`)
Raw 1.2 coefficient, effective 1.08. Interval 0.5 s. Chain: 4 targets, range 12 m, falloff x0.75 per hop. Proc 0.8 direct, 0.4/0.2/0.1 on hops. Projectile speed 120, 3 degree aim assist.

| Case | Calc | DPS coeff |
|---|---|---|
| 1 target | 1.08 / 0.5 | 216%/s |
| 4 targets in chain range | 1.08 x (1 + .75 + .5625 + .4219) = 2.953 per bolt, /0.5 | 590%/s total (216 on first) |
| Proc units/s | single 0.8 x 2 = 1.6; 4 targets 1.5 x 2 = 3.0 | |

Vanilla (V): Commando Double Tap about 360%/s (given), proc 1.0 each. At equal body damage HS single target is 60% of Commando; at L1 with 15 vs 12 base it is 75%. In packs the chain makes it better (about 1.6x Commando total at L1 damage equivalence). No cooldown. Attack speed scales fire rate linearly. Verdict: fair to slightly low single target; fine.

### Secondary: Stormspear (`StormspearTuning`, `SpearFeedbackPolicy`, `SpearBurstPolicy`)
Direct = 0.9 x (3.5 + 10.5c) x (1 - 0.1c), c = charge 0..1. Charge 2.0 s at 1x (Open Circuit crown x2.5, so 0.8 s). Stock 1, recharge 5 x 1.2 = 6 s. Per the changelog the recharge is paused while holding, so a full-charge cycle is about 2 + 6 = 8 s (tap cycle about 6 s). Proc 1.0 direct, 0.5 burst.

Extras: conductor on struck enemy, 4 ticks x 0.75 s, 2 victims per tick, 0.2 to 0.35 x 0.9 = 18% to 31.5% per tick (negligible, ~5% of the cast). A fully held spear also lands an "ordinary" Thunderbolt: 0.9 x 10 x 0.425 = 382% direct + 50% splash r3 (proc 1.0). If the bank is full (5 charges) the strike is "funded": 765% direct + splash (code: `PrayerStrikeSnapshot`, `FundedStrikeMultiplier .85`, ordinary half of that). The spear is competitive on single target and notably strong on crowds because every enemy within 10 m takes up to full-direct damage with item procs. That is the secondary's real power.

## 3. Gaze of the Hollow (code: `GazeTuning`, `GazeReleaseTuning`, `GazeServer`, `GazeFuelPulse`, `GazeState`)

### Cast structure
Wind-up 1.0 s (no damage, armor already on), beam 7.0 s fixed (release trial; the config "Beam seconds" slider is now inert), end 0.4 s. Cooldown 12 s starts at skill end. Cycle = 1 + 7 + 0.4 + 12 = 20.4 s, uptime 41% (the old 4 s no-fuel cast was 17.4 s).
Armor +30 (total 45, 31% reduction) from wind-up to beam end. Hover 6 m, sink 0.25 m/s, drift 0.3 (reported 0.33 in the trial doc, I did not find the 10% in `GazeTuning`). Not invulnerable: ranged and flying enemies still reach 4 to 6 m. Primary, secondary and utility are replaced during the cast (other combat skills unavailable), so Gaze output is not additive with spear and bolt.
Gaze is exempt from the 0.9 multiplier and Gaze damage is `DamageSource.Special`.

### Continuous beam (all code)
| Component | Per-hit | Rate | Proc | Scales with attack speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core | 100% per tick (5.0 x 0.2) | 5 ticks/s => 500%/s, pierces all enemies in 1.5 m radius to 60 m | 0.5 | yes, ticks = 5 x AS, floor 0.05 s so cap at 4x AS; crit rolled per tick |
| Impact splash | 50% of a tick to enemies not hit by core, radius 3 x reach (0.4 to 1.6 over the cast = 1.2 to 4.8 m) | per tick | 0.3 | yes |
| Forks | 100% to up to 2 distinct enemies within 8 x reach (3.2 to 12.8 m), LoS | every 0.5 s | 0.3 | no |
| Fork chain | 60% of fork damage to one more enemy within 6 x reach | with each fork | 0.2 | no |

Core over 7 s single target: 3500%. Forks: 1 enemy in range gives 100% per 0.5 s = 200%/s (no chain target), 3+ enemies give 2 x (1 + .6) = 320% per 0.5 s = 640%/s (more than the core). Forks do not exclude core victims. Splash: a 3 m crowd next to the impact takes up to 250%/s each.

| Beam only, 7 s | Single target | 4 to 6 clustered enemies (core hits ~2, forks reach all) |
|---|---|---|
| Core | 3500% | 3500% on each enemy in the line |
| Forks | 1400% (1 fork per volley) | 14 x 3.2 = 4480% spread over the cluster |
| Splash | 0 | ~1750% each (35 ticks x 50%) on non-line enemies at the impact |

Attack speed scaling of the core: AS 1 = 3500, AS 2 = 7000, AS 3 = 10500, AS 4+ = 14000% per target per cast. Forks and surges do not scale, so fork share falls, core share rises.

### Surges (hold/release; `GazeReleaseTuning`, `GazeFuelPulse`)
Hold Primary: 1 charge ready immediately, 2 at 0.45 s, 3 at 0.90 s (max 3 per surge, release spends them). 0.65 s recovery. Damage = body damage x 4.0 x charges to the primary hit plus every eligible enemy on the ground route within the spread radius (3.2 to 12.8 m, up to 8 victims), arrival 0.4 to 0.9 s after release (travel + spread). Same damage to each victim (no falloff). Rolls one crit per surge. Proc coefficient 0 (so no item procs and no Static from surges; storm guard also prevents Static). Heals 1% max health per spent charge, up to 5% (native `Heal`). Surges are fixed damage: not scaled by attack speed. Charges = entry bank (max 5, one group of 3 and one of 2, i.e. at most 2 surges fit comfortably in 7 s). New charges earned during the cast go to reserve and cannot be spent in the same cast.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 3500 | 1400 | 0 | 4900% | 240%/s | 583%/s |
| 1 | 3500 | 1400 | 400 | 5300% | 260% | 631% |

| 4 | 3500 | 1400 | 1600 | 6500% | 319% | 774% |
| 5 | 3500 | 1400 | 2000 | 6900% | 338% | 821% |

Per victim in a pack, surges are 2000% each x up to 8 victims = 16,000% for 5 charges. Pack (6 enemies), rough: 3500 x 2 (line) + 4480 forks + 3500 splash + 12,000 surges, about 27,000% per cast, about 1300%/s over the cycle.
At AS 3 the single-target core is 10,500% so surges are 16% of a no-fork cast.

Comparison to the shipped 1.1.1/old early-spend (from `1.2-gaze-research.md`): old full bank 8,898% core+pulses over 27.4 s cycle = 325%/s, old zero-fuel 2,000% over 17.4 s = 115%/s (excluding forks). New zero-fuel (core only) 3500/20.4 = 172%/s (+49%), new full bank 270%/s (core+surge, -17% vs old full bank). So the trial raises the floor and trims the ceiling, which is a good direction. Fork contribution roughly doubles because 7 s gives 14 volleys instead of 8 (and the old 14 s cast gave 28).

## 4. Passive: Storm / Static / Electrocute / Thunderbolt

Code (`StormServer`, `KitTuning`): Static gain per hit = clamp(damageDealt / (victim max HP x 0.25), 0.06, 1) x procCoefficient (x1.5 on crit). Hits with proc 0 add nothing, except Open Circuit pulses (weight 0.3). Decay starts 2 s after last hit at 0.5/s. At 100% the enemy Electrocutes: 0.5 s stun (bosses excluded) + Shocked 3 s (all damage x1.15), 4 s Electrocute immunity, +1 Charge to the bank (cap 5), and Pop: 150% x up to 2 neighbours in 6 m, proc 0.3, no crit, not reduced by the 0.9 factor, +0.15 Static to those neighbours (cascade depth max 3). Hard cap 4 Electrocutes per second. Death discharge: an enemy dying with at least 50% Static pops and counts as a charge.

Value per Electrocute: Pop 300% (two targets), Shocked +15% to damage on that enemy for 3 s, +1 charge. Five charges buy a funded spear strike 765% + 50% splash r3, or entry fuel for Gaze surges (5 charges = up to 2000% per victim x 8 victims). By value, a charge is worth about 150 to 450% via spear but up to 400% x targets via Gaze: inconsistent sinks. Gaze is much the better charge sink.

Typical fight estimate (my model, not measured): stage 1 to 3, 10 to 20 enemies over 30 s. Early (level 1, 15 damage) one Arc Bolt (16 dmg) is about 0.65 Static on an 80 hp enemy, so 2 bolts Electrocute it; expect 10 to 20 Electrocutes per such fight = 3000 to 6000% pop damage, 2 to 4 charge banks, and Shocked up on many targets. Against bosses (1000+ hp) the minimum gain 0.06 x proc 0.8 = 0.048 means about 21 bolt hits (10 s) per Electrocute. The passive therefore front-loads: very strong in clear phases (20 to 30% of total damage), quiet on bosses.

Runaway checks:
- Caps: 4 Electrocutes/s, 4 s immunity per enemy, cascade depth 3, pop 2 targets x 0.15 Static (cannot chain without pre-built Static), storm damage guard blocks pop/Thunderbolt/Gaze surge from refilling Static directly. Good.
- Item loops: pop proc 0.3 can spawn ATG/Ukulele/Behemoth effects; damage dealt by later frames (missiles) is outside the guard and builds Static (proc > 0). Expected loop gain is well under 1 (ATG 10% x 0.3 per stack per pop target, and missiles carry low proc), so no true runaway at normal stacks; stack 10+ ATG/Ukulele with Gaze at 3x AS+ is the realistic stress case.
- Gaze self-funding: Gaze core builds Static fast (5 ticks x 0.5), creating Electrocutes in a pack, refilling reserve to 5 as surges spend, so every cast in a crowd has a full 5-charge bank. That is by design but means the 2000% x 8 victims surge is effectively free in sustained pack fights. A boss-only fight is the check (bank may not refill).
- The Shocked 1.15x applies to all attackers including allies and DoTs.

## 7. Recommended changes

Author philosophy: keep mechanics strong, trim outliers. These keep the surge/hold-release idea intact and only restore parity with the 1.1.1 power budget that the 4 s to 7 s duration change inflated.

### Do for 1.2
| # | Field | Current | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `7. Gaze` Proc coefficient (`GazeTuning.ProcCoefficient`) | 0.5 | 0.30 | Duration 4 to 7 s gives 75% more proc rolls per cast. 0.3 x 7 = 2.1 vs 0.5 x 4 = 2.0 keeps item output per cast flat versus 1.1.1. Static from Gaze slows about 40% but still fills in packs (the min gain 0.06 floor still applies); watch boss cadence |
| 2 | `7. Gaze` Fork damage (`GazeTuning.ForkDamage`) | 1.0 | 0.70 | 14 volleys now instead of 8 (+75%); in packs forks (640%/s) already beat the core and do not scale with AS. 0.7 x 14 = 9.8 vs 8 x 1.0 keeps +22% instead of +75% |
| 3 | Bind the trial constants to config: `GazeReleaseTuning.BeamSeconds`, `DamagePerCharge`, `SecondsPerExtraCharge`, `MaximumLoaded` | const | KitConfig floats | Needed to retune at all: the shipped "Beam seconds" slider is inert in the trial and Gaze balance cannot be adjusted by playtesters. Not a value change |
| 4 | Splash proc (`GazeTuning.SplashProc`) | 0.3 const | 0.2 (make config) | Fires every tick for crowd members next to the impact, scales with AS, and now lasts 7 s |

Everything else in the kit I would leave for 1.2: Arc Bolt, Arc Step, Open Circuit, spear direct damage, passive coefficients.
