# Gaze: crown-driven charge feedback

October 5, 2026. Private follow-up on `codex/gaze-release-120`, after Stuart's quick playtest of hold/release.

## Requested direction

Keep exploring hold/release, but communicate charging through animation and beam VFX. Remove the word-heavy charge panel. Make the physical crown in front of the Saint expand during preparation and release that shape down the beam. Remove the floating circle-and-bolt ammunition icons during Gaze.

## Implementation

- The actual crown's four arc docks smoothly expand to 1.6 times their uncharged radius at full preparation, keeping the character, root scale and metal thickness unchanged. One/two/three acknowledged charges drive increasing expansion; transitions ease over 0.18 seconds.
- Four open electrical arcs follow the live crown radius. Releasing lets the metal crown settle over 0.16 seconds and projects a matching four-part energy ring along the existing frozen surge path. The ring expands slightly on departure, follows authoritative travel time and stays within the existing damage corridor. It fades at arrival. The old broad pulse sleeve, outline and forks are hidden in this mode so they cannot cover the ring.
- Gaze's floating charge icons, icon intake trails, travelling bolt glyph and added text/pip/timer panel are suppressed in the release trial. Ordinary charge display outside Gaze and native skill stock remain available. Ending Gaze does not briefly reintroduce floating icons during its return animation.
- Existing beam, ground waves, confirmed-hit feedback and authored release audio remain. Preparation does not fabricate damage or confirmed-hit cues.
- Charge timing, damage, seven-second duration, movement, refunds and healing are unchanged from the first trial.

The presentation lives in `GazeEmpowermentFx.Crown.cs`, with the existing `GazeCrownMount` retaining ownership of physical transforms. The rejected `GazeReleaseHud.cs` has been removed. `HS_GAZE_BASELINE=1` still selects the older comparison route; it is not a public multiplayer configuration.
