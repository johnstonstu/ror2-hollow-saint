### Two corrections that must not be re-learned

Both were found by subagents and confirmed by the coordinator against the
GameLibs assembly. Getting either wrong costs a runtime failure, not a compile error:

1. **`EntityStates.EntityState` is a plain class, NOT a MonoBehaviour.** States
   cannot be added as child GameObjects. They are instantiated from a `Type` via
   `R2API.ContentAddition.AddEntityState`, and must be registered before
   `EntityStateCatalog.Init()`.
2. **`SerializableEntityStateType` IS constructible** and lives in namespace
   `EntityStates`, **not** `RoR2`. A "type not found" result usually means a
   wrong-namespace lookup, not a missing type.

Type-name traps: it is `RoR2.Skills.SkillDef` (not `RoR2.SkillDef`),
`EntityStates.EntityState` (no `RoR2` prefix), and the types live in the **nuget
GameLibs** package, not the game's `RoR2.dll`. `ilspycmd` needs `C:/` paths —
`/c/` MSYS paths fail with "file does not exist". Decompiled method bodies show
`throw null;`; that is a decompiler artefact, not real behaviour.
