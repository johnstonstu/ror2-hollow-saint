## Three directions

| Direction | Silhouette and equipment | Character impression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storm Salvager | Stocky insulated suit, asymmetric copper coil backpack, heavy boots, charged gauntlet | Resourceful industrial survivor |
| Living Circuit | Lean angular automaton, large forearms, shoulder conductor ring, cyan core | Strange machine built to channel storms |
| Tempest Nomad | Mask, short split poncho, ceramic armor, copper forearm rings | Agile science-fantasy wanderer |

Favor large readable shapes, a visible firing hand, and a manageable humanoid rig.
Start with a concept sheet; after choosing a direction, develop front/side/back
references, a Blender blockout, rigging, and animation. A generated image is visual
reference, not a rigged or game-ready model.

## Saved Higgsfield prompt

Create one polished game character concept exploration sheet, landscape 16:9,
three equally spaced full-body ORIGINAL lightning survivor designs for a Risk of
Rain 2 fan mod. Stylized low-poly science-fantasy, chunky readable silhouettes,
matte materials, restrained surface detail, strong shapes readable at third-person
camera distance, pale warm gray background, subtle grounding shadows.

All three should communicate chain lightning emitted from one hand, not a gun,
with a small elegant electric arc, but keep anatomy and costume visible and
practical for Blender modeling and a humanoid rig.

LEFT, label '01 / STORM SALVAGER': stocky astronaut-electrician, insulated cream
work suit, large asymmetric copper coil backpack, heavy boots, dark opaque visor,
ochre accents, energized gauntlet.

MIDDLE, label '02 / LIVING CIRCUIT': lean humanoid automaton, dark graphite angular
armor, floating but connected circular conductor behind shoulders, bright cyan
core, oversized forearms and slim legs, no human face.

RIGHT, label '03 / TEMPEST NOMAD': agile mysterious masked wanderer, short split
teal poncho above the knees, ceramic armor, copper rings on forearms, pale blue
electrical accents; no long cloth train.

Distinct silhouettes and personalities, cohesive art direction with bold painterly
blocks of light and graphic shading. Top title 'CHAIN LIGHTNING / SURVIVOR
EXPLORATIONS'. Only those labels, no extra body text. No existing characters,
no photorealism, no intricate greebles. Concept art, not a finished game asset.
