## What is selected and what remains open

Selected: option A's slender body, blank mask, ivory/dark/copper/cyan palette,
small round core, segmented circular halo, hand-fired chain lightning, and
hits building charge. The user has not finalized the name or supporting abilities.

Current construction proposals: four independent halo arcs with shorter lower
sections, a simple back yoke, five digits per hand, long mid-shin tabard, and a
rear charge node. Refinement follows the selected LEFT A full-body silhouette;
the earlier complete ring and short tabard were rejected blockout inferences.

## Consistency review before 3D

- Several full-body images subdivide the halo into more pieces; use the four-quarter halo detail as the proposed construction reference, pending user preference.
- The Open Circuit pose adds a second apparent halo ring and extra floating fragments. Treat it as expressive ability exploration, not approved geometry.
- The first charge sheet repeats front-like views despite requesting rear views. Use the dedicated turnaround for rear silhouette, with the same consistency caveat.
- Tabard length, shoulder plates, hands, yoke attachment, and rear node placement vary. Reconcile these in a final construction drawing or blockout rather than tracing all sheets literally.
- Keep glow localized so the body, enemies, and chain path remain readable at game-camera distance.

## Generation record

All artwork used the built-in image-generation tool with the selected A figure
as a reference. Exact prompts are saved in the sibling `prompts` directory.
Higgsfield's connected plan was checked and remains free; the earlier image
generation request required Basic or higher. No subscription/trial was started.
No 3D model, animations, or gameplay implementation has been created.
