## 01 - Storm Rigger

A shipyard electrician surviving with equipment pushed far beyond its intended
limits. Stocky orange work suit, asymmetric cable reel, ceramic insulators, and
an oversized copper clamp gauntlet.

- Primary animation: close the clamp to strike a short arc at the aimed enemy.
- Charge: insulators light in sequence; at full charge the clamp opens wide and arcs bridge its jaws.
- Possible supporting skill: throw a grounding rod that acts as a temporary chain relay.
- Modeling assessment: relatively straightforward humanoid with rigid accessories.
- Strength: practical personality and an immediate visual explanation for lightning.

## 02 - Stormrunner

A nimble masked wanderer who wears a portable lightning conductor. Narrow frame,
short teal rain cape, ceramic guards, and two swept-back rods above the shoulders.

- Primary animation: quick wrist flicks or open-palm snaps, suitable for rapid attacks.
- Charge: backpack coils illuminate; a visible arc joins the rods at full charge.
- Possible supporting skill: a quick dash leaving a short-lived charged afterimage.
- Modeling assessment: humanoid base, with a few cape bones and rigid backpack pieces.
- Strength: agile personality and clear rear-view charge feedback.

## 03 - Hollow Saint

An artificial ceramic vessel containing a living storm. Blank face, slender limbs,
exposed electrical core, and a segmented copper halo framing the shoulders.

- Primary animation: a restrained pointing gesture; electricity forks outward from the fingers.
- Charge: halo segments energize and align as the storm becomes harder to contain.
- Possible supporting skill: mark an enemy as a conductor, prioritizing it for subsequent chains.
- Modeling assessment: moderate; conventional humanoid core, extra halo transforms, emission effects.
- Strength: lightning is part of its identity and body, with a striking silhouette.
- Simplify the generated rib details and hanging fabric if this direction is selected.

## 04 - Storm Hermit

A small alien scavenger living beneath an oversized salvaged insulator shell.
Hunched triangular silhouette, teal limbs, tiny peeking head, and a long conductor claw.

- Primary animation: a forward claw jab that releases a controlled electrical snap.
- Charge: shell sections light up; the three prongs connect with an arc at full charge.
- Possible supporting skill: place a charged shell fragment as a temporary chain relay.
- Modeling assessment: moderate; rigid shell is simple, while hunched proportions require a custom gait.
- Strength: the most creature-like direction and a memorable silhouette from behind.

## Shared combat questions to explore after choosing a silhouette

The user has confirmed aimed chain lightning and charge from hits. The prior
design brief's damage values, automatic empowered shot, charge thresholds, and
remaining skills are proposals rather than locked requirements.

- Does ordinary fire feel like deliberate electrical snaps or a rapid sequence of little arcs?
- Does full charge empower the next shot automatically, or should the player choose when to release it?
- Should the other skills steer chain paths, spend charge, or help reposition?

## Recommendation

Develop Hollow Saint if the user wants an eerie being made of lightning; develop
Storm Hermit if a strange alien scavenger is more appealing. Storm Rigger is the
clearest practical industrial alternative. Mix selected features freely before
producing a clean front/side/back model reference and a Blender blockout.
