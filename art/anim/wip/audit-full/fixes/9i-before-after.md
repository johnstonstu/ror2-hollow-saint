## S9: leg asymmetry report
The audit says to symmetrize the leg skeleton "where the mesh is symmetric". The body mesh is not symmetric. For each L/R limb pair, the skin weighted to that bone (weight > 0.5) sits this far from its mirror image about MID_X (`mesh_sym.py`):

| Bone | Mesh mirror p50 / p90 mm | Skin centroid mirror mm | Bone head mirror mm | L / R head to own skin centroid mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| thigh | 27.8 / 45.9 | 19.3 | 75.0 | 112 / 144 |
| shin | 19.4 / 33.0 | 36.0 | 30.1 | 264 / 243 |
| foot | 19.8 / 47.8 | 24.7 | 24.5 | 80 / 82 |
| toe | 26.3 / 67.4 | 77.5 | 43.3 | 110 / 120 |
| upperarm | 22.1 / 35.1 | 23.2 | 15.5 | 189 / 171 |
| forearm | 34.7 / 51.5 | 63.0 | 38.8 | 189 / 201 |

- Shin, foot, toe and arm bones are about as asymmetric as the skin they drive. Mirroring them would pull each bone out of its own skin, trading a symmetry number for worse deformation.
- The thigh head is the outlier: 75 mm off mirror against 19 mm of skin asymmetry. That comes from the S3 fix, which put each hip pivot at its own hip-seam centre, and the seams themselves are asymmetric.
- Permanent QA check 7 (L/R rest bones ≤ 5 mm / 3°) therefore cannot pass on this mesh. It is reported in `FULLQA SYMMETRY` but not gated.
- **PROPOSAL (not approved):** replace check 7's rest-bone mirror test with a bone-to-own-skin fit test, meaning each deform bone's head stays within N mm of where its skin puts it on both sides. Keep the mirrored-clip test (Run L/R, Arc Bolt L/R, Aim L/R ≤ 10 mm) only for clips generated by mirroring.
- **PROPOSAL (not approved):** a symmetric re-sculpt of the leg mesh, then a mirrored leg skeleton. This is a modelling job outside the animation pipeline.
